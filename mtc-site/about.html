About Morgan Trading Company

Northern Michigan's trusted destination to buy, sell, and trade the things that hold value.

Morgan Trading Company is a full-service buy, sell & trade business with two locations in Gaylord and Alpena, Michigan. We're a licensed FFL firearms dealer, a precious metals and coin dealer, and specialists in jewelry, diamonds, luxury watches, and designer handbags.

What sets us apart is simple: honest appraisals, fair prices, and real expertise across every category we deal in. Whether you're turning gold into cash, trading up to a new watch, or reserving a piece on layaway, you'll be treated fairly and paid honestly.
