Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying, selling, and trading with us.

Do I need an appointment?

Nope — just walk in during business hours (Mon–Sat, 10AM–6PM) at either location. We'll appraise your item for free, on the spot.

How much does an appraisal cost?

Appraisals are always free, with no obligation to sell. Bring your item in and we'll tell you what it's worth.

What do you buy?

Firearms & ammunition (we're a licensed FFL dealer), gold, silver & platinum, coins & bullion, diamonds & fine jewelry, luxury watches, designer handbags, and quality general merchandise — tools, electronics, musical instruments and more.

How do you decide what to pay for gold and silver?

Offers are based on live spot-market prices, purity, and weight. You can watch current spot prices on our Live Display, and we'll gladly show you exactly how your offer is calculated.

How does layaway work?

Put down just 10% to reserve an item, then pay the balance in comfortable monthly installments — no credit check. See our Layaway page for details.

What do I need to sell or transfer a firearm?

A valid government-issued ID, plus a background check where required by law. As a licensed FFL dealer, we handle every firearm transaction in full compliance with federal and Michigan law.

Do you offer trade-ins?

Yes — trade what you own toward anything in our showcase, and combine a trade with cash or layaway. Learn about trading →
