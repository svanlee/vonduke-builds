Disclosures & Policies

Important information about how we operate and the licenses we hold.

Licensing

Morgan Trading Company is a Federally Licensed Firearms Dealer (FFL) and operates as a licensed precious metal and coin dealer in the State of Michigan. All firearm sales, purchases, and transfers are conducted in full compliance with federal and Michigan law, including required background checks.

Appraisals & Offers

All appraisals are provided free of charge and without obligation. Offers are based on current market conditions, item authenticity, and condition at the time of appraisal, and are valid only for that visit. Precious-metal offers are tied to live spot-market prices and may change throughout the day.

Layaway

Our layaway program requires a minimum deposit of 10% of the item's price to reserve it, with the balance payable in monthly installments. No credit check is required. Full terms, eligible items, and any applicable fees are available in-store. Deposits and payment terms may vary by item and value.

Firearms Transactions

All firearm purchases and transfers require a valid government-issued ID and successful completion of a background check where required by law. We reserve the right to refuse any transaction that does not meet legal requirements. Buyers are responsible for knowing and complying with applicable local, state, and federal firearms laws.

Authenticity

We make every effort to verify the authenticity of luxury goods, jewelry, watches, and collectibles. Descriptions reflect our good-faith assessment at the time of sale. Customers are encouraged to inspect all items in person before purchase.

Pricing & Availability

Inventory changes frequently and items shown on this website or on our Live Display are illustrative of the categories we carry; specific items are subject to availability and prior sale. Prices are subject to change without notice.
